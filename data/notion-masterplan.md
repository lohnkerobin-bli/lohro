## 3. Two-Track Strategy

- **Spur A — OSCAR** (structural, long-term, mechanical): "Ein Kurzfilm gewinnt einen qualifying festival award → Oscar-Shortlist bis 2028–2030."
- **Spur B — PERSONAL BRAND** (emotional, mid-term, public): "Lohro wächst bis die Brand selbst Türen öffnet (Schauspieler, Komponisten, Produzenten, später Kinobesucher)."
- The tracks interlock — the YouTube documentation of the Oscar journey IS brand content — "Aber die Spuren sind nicht dasselbe Ding. Jede braucht eigene Ziele."
- Time rule: "3h/Tag Oscar-Track muss **wirklich** Oscar-Track sein." Daily reel = muscle A; shooting a 15-minute short = muscle B; both need their own training time.

### Track A: Mentor track — Priority 1
- 30-name target list in 3 categories: (1) Swiss short-film directors (Solothurn/Winterthur winners 2023–2026, Locarno Pardi di Domani, Max Ophüls), (2) Swiss arthouse feature directors (Schweizer Filmpreis Best Director last 5 years, Quartz environment, Locarno Main Competition), (3) DPs and producers (Langfilm, Filmgerberei, Hugofilm)
- 4-line cold-mail formula: concrete film/scene/observation → 1-sentence intro → concrete craft/strategy question → coffee invitation (Robin pays)
- Expected conversion: 1 in 5 replies, 1 in 3 agrees to meet → **2 meetings from 30 mails**
- After meeting: never ask for mentorship; send result-updates at 4 weeks, 3 months, then a 20-min call ask at 6 months. "Mentorschaft entsteht nicht durch Fragen. Durch Ergebnisse."

### Track A: Festival strategy
- Ground rule: "Premiere-Status bei Top-Festivals ist heilig. Falsche Reihenfolge = Disqualifikation."
- **Tier 1 (world premiere):** Clermont-Ferrand (Jan, submit Oct), Locarno Pardi di Domani (Aug, submit Apr), Sundance (Jan, submit Aug), Berlinale Shorts (Feb, submit Oct)
- **Tier 2 (qualifying):** Palm Springs Shortfest, Tribeca, SXSW, Aspen Shortsfest, Tampere
- **Tier 3 (Swiss/DACH premiere):** Solothurner Filmtage, Kurzfilmtage Winterthur, Max Ophüls Preis, Oberhausen
- Sequence: attempt Tier-1 world premiere → then parallel Tier-2 submissions → Swiss premiere in Solothurn/Winterthur for domestic standing + funding applications. Check oscars.org qualifying list annually.
- **Budget per festival campaign: CHF 3'000–8'000** (fees CHF 30–80 × 15–20 = CHF 600–1'500; travel CHF 500–2'000 per festival; subtitling CHF 500–1'500; DCP CHF 300–800)

## 5. Content Formats, Workflows & Editor Bible (LOHRO Master Plan)

### Mission & brand
> "Every person is created by God and gifted with unique talents. Through cinematic storytelling, Lohro inspires people to fully embrace their gifts and realize their full potential — with a 'what-if-it-goes-right' mentality."

- Core values: Faith first, Optimism ("What if it goes right?"), Craft over gear, Authenticity, Excellence.
- Audience: aspiring filmmakers "beyond beginner but not yet professional".
- Visual DNA: early Kanye West visuals, Ratatouille, Severance, Breaking Bad, Interstellar. Brand colors: plant green, dark red, dark blue (accents only). Camera: Sony FX3.

### Editor Bible — key rules
- Voice: direct, personal ("I struggled with this too"), slightly unfinished, short sentences, meaningful pauses.
- **Golden rule: "cut on emotion, not on grammar."**
- Pacing: cold open cuts every 2–4s; concept section every 5–10s; emotional payoff slower. Jump cuts only for energy, never to hide dead time. Never cut mid-breath on a key word, a nonverbal reaction, or a tension pause.
- Color: cinematic, slightly desaturated, warm shadows (dark amber, not blue-black), natural warm skin tones, controlled highlights. References: Interstellar, Breaking Bad S5, early A24. "Think *Interstellar* warmth meets *Severance* precision."
- Music: supports emotion, never announces it; ducked to **15–20% of natural volume** under speech; natural ambience as glue; Hans Zimmer-adjacent, "introspective, cinematic, slightly melancholic but hopeful".
- Text: clean/modern/minimal; captions match speech rhythm; subtle motion only; on-screen text must add, not repeat.
- Thumbnails — all four rules required: (1) image tells a story, (2) Robin's face with clear readable emotion, (3) one main focus, (4) coherent color concept.
- Viral formula arc (every YouTube video): Cold Open (0–30s, hook) → Universal Problem → Name the Concept → Concrete Examples → Emotional Payoff → CTA.

### Weekly workflow
- **Monday — Plan & Create:** review monthly calendar, finalize voiceover script, set the week's 3 non-negotiable content tasks; afternoon: film talking head, batch Instagram/Reels.
- **Friday — Review & Schedule:** review week's content, edit or hand off with notes, schedule next week's Instagram + captions, update monthly YouTube planner, thumbnail concept, weekly debrief.
- **Weekends:** optional creative reserve (b-roll, drafting, journaling) — never admin.
- **Daily non-negotiables:** Bible reading + morning routine, goal review/manifestation, sport — before opening any app.

### Planning systems
- YouTube: plan 4–6 weeks ahead; **1 video/week**, fully finished 48h before upload; status pipeline `IDEE → SKRIPT → GEDREHT → SCHNITT → BEREIT → LIVE`; per-video checklist (script read-aloud test, strong cold open, FX3 talking head, b-roll list, editor brief, thumbnail, description/tags, end screen, scheduled).
- Instagram: **daily posting challenge**, plan 2+ weeks ahead. Five content pillars: behind the scenes, craft tips, personal/identity (faith, Way to Oscar), client work highlights, repurposed YouTube. Weekly cadence: Mon BTS, Tue craft tip, Wed personal/journey, Thu client work, Fri repurposed YouTube/Reel, Sat experimental, Sun reflection/gratitude/quote.
- Scripts: written to be spoken; ≤20 words without a beat; explicit `[Pause]`/`[Beat]`/energy cues; start with a scene, not a thesis; "The first line is the most important line in the entire video." Production flow: IDEA → SCRIPT DRAFT → READ ALOUD TEST → REVISE → FILM → EDIT BRIEF → EDIT → REVIEW → THUMBNAIL → SCHEDULE.
